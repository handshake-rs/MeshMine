Phase 3 boundaries
==================
Implemented source behavior includes:

- validated non-active header/block/body retention;
- separate best-header and active-best-block bindings;
- strictly greater-work promotion and equal-work first-seen preservation;
- active-to-candidate reorganization planning;
- ancestry, canonical-disconnect, contiguous-connect, body, status, and work
  validation before activation;
- one immutable base snapshot, one read-your-writes overlay, and one durable
  batch for a complete replacement;
- a final replacement-chainwork check before commit;
- gated restart recovery on native-experimental regtest/simnet only;
- durable-chain startup invariants and expanded diagnostics.

This does not claim complete HSD historical fork-choice parity, production
mainnet authority, complete script/contextual covenant/name/Urkel consensus, or
live P2P synchronization.
