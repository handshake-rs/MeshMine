not mean production-ready or independently audited. This matrix groups the
normative `MUST`/`MUST NOT` statements by enforcement boundary. The release

| §15 parent, stale work, and reorganization | Local pass | Every share requires a participant-local `ParentChainOracle`; the `hsd-cli` adapter checks canonicality, height, nTime, and chainwork. Non-research commands bind an absolute canonical executable identity/digest, reject symlink, unowned, or unsafe writable Unix paths, re-attest immediately before each spawn, and execute the reopened descriptor rather than a newly resolved pathname. JavaScript adapter commands likewise re-attest and descriptor-pin an absolute canonical effective-user/root-owned, non-writable source directory beneath no unsafe writable ancestor; non-writable ancestors may have another owner. The imported descendants remain mutable and lack a full-tree content commitment, while the Node runtime also remains an external artifact control. Optional static-profile daemon ingress also enforces settlement threshold plus durable certificate sequence linkage. A parent journal record is immutable audit evidence only: conditional head advancement atomically writes a network/sequence/object-bound acceptance marker, bounded recovery repairs markers only on the verified head chain, and unmarked losing race siblings cannot enter gossip migration or intent recovery. A live canonical Disconnect commits its raw event, exact height/hash MMDB tombstone, earliest parent/session markers and closure rows, accepted descendant-suffix rewind, and exact canonical-sequence (`MMPC`) deletions in one conditional transaction. Exact marker/tombstone CAS loss retries the whole transition and retains the earliest event sequence. Startup validates the complete raw event chain before writing, aggregates Disconnects by block, and performs one parent plus one MaskSession evidence scan per complete CAS attempt; payout evidence is not normalized. Immutable historical evidence remains, while a replacement reuses the orphan sequence so peers that missed it can accept the replacement and successor in order. Startup also joins every retained, validated orphan-parent marker against retained sessions, repairing the legacy case where an older build had already rewound the head but omitted a descendant-session closure. Parent and reorg-closed-share delivery tombstones never manufacture peer ACK evidence. Gateway grace rules are objective. Before listening and at bounded intervals, the supervised controller reconciles canonical connects/disconnects, rolls plan payment back, invalidates entropy plans and parent certificates, durably closes affected sessions, retains share/body evidence, and recovers legacy raw-event effects after interruption. Choosing a retained horizon that covers active obligations and defining a bounded parent checkpoint and resumable deep-reorg repair fence remain operator/protocol work. |

For the §15 result, parent admission conditions the exact `(network, height, block_hash)` MMDB tombstone absent, so a candidate that passed the oracle before a concurrent Disconnect cannot resurrect stale canonical state. A malformed or gapped raw canonical-event chain produces no derived reorg writes. This admission-race fence is not the still-open reversible parent checkpoint/resumable deep-reorg repair fence, and the atomic Disconnect result changes no §26 gate.

The software therefore implements the staged local research profile while
intentionally refusing a production/mainnet claim. `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` lists
every unresolved security or wire decision.
